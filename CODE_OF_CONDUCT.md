# Code of Conduct

Woolly follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short, be kind and assume good intent. Harassment, insults, personal attacks and publishing someone's private information aren't welcome in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else Woolly is worked on.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, email the contact address on [usewoolly.com/terms](https://usewoolly.com/terms). Reports are read by the maintainer and kept private.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits or issues, and may warn or ban anyone who breaks this code, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
